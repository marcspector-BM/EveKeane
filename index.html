A Star So Far from Heaven is a true story. A woman finds a single letter written long ago, and the daring decision that follows carries her on a life-changing journey.

It is a memoir of people, places and relationships, told with candor and humor. Whether you are on your own path of self-discovery or simply want a read that stays with you, this book is for you.

About Evelyn Goldfinch

Evelyn Goldfinch is a musician who carves out a living in a small town in the hills of Pennsylvania, where she lives with her two worshiped and completely adorable cats, Moses and Mirabel.

Her writing draws on the quiet beauty of rural life, the complexity of human connection, and the search for meaning in an often chaotic world. Her voice is both fierce and vulnerable, and her stories stay with you long after the final page.

Author and musician, Pennsylvania, USA
