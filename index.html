About the Author

Evelyn Goldfinch

Evelyn Goldfinch is a musician who carves out a living in a small town in the hills of Pennsylvania, where she resides with her two worshiped and completely adorable cats, Moses and Mirabel.

Her writing draws from a life rich with experience — the quiet beauty of rural life, the complexity of human connection, and the search for meaning in an often chaotic world. With a voice that is both fierce and vulnerable, Evelyn invites readers into her world, offering stories that resonate long after the final page.

About the Book

A Star So Far from Heaven

A profoundly moving memoir that speaks directly to the soul through reflections on people, places, and relationships.

A Star So Far from Heaven is a true story about what began as an ordinary cleaning day, the discovery of a single letter written long ago and the events of one woman's daring decision and life-changing journey that followed.

Whether you are navigating your own path of self-discovery or simply seeking an evocative and thought-provoking read, this book is sure to leave a lasting impression.
